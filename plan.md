## Delivery ownership and boundaries

Finance/business review checks whether the decision and uncertainty are useful to an investor and understandable to judges. Engineering maintains numerical methods, explicit scope, evidence and execution traces. Both reviews use the same fictional cases and measured application outputs.

No outstanding work item authorizes real trading, personal advice, fabricated evidence or a silent relaxation of investor constraints. A missing external service or source is a documented blocker, not a reason to manufacture a passing result.
