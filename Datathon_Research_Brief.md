## Design Direction

**A single reallocation can improve geographic diversification, deepen overlap among underlying holdings, and increase losses under a specified set of shocks.** Build the prototype around a specific decision. Given an existing portfolio, a defined source of funds, and a hypothetical trade, show which risks improve, which increase, and which investor constraint or market assumption changes the result.

The organizers' finance workshop illustrates this decision with Canadian equities, ZEQT, and XSP. Existing holdings-analysis products also provide fund look-through and overlap checks. The proposed competition differentiator is to **show the trade's different risk effects, investor constraints, decision thresholds, and verifiable evidence in one interaction**. The cases, agent count, and metrics in this brief are design options. ## Recalculate a Trade from Issuer Disclosures

Use disclosures with a common valuation date of **2026-06-30**. BMO reports ZEQT's geographic allocation as 24.7% Canada, 49.3% U.S., 16.6% international developed markets, and 9.4% emerging markets. ZSP's fair value was C$400.775M against ZEQT net assets of C$880.582M, or roughly **45.51%**. BlackRock's disclosure for the same date shows that XSP's IVV holding represented about **99.9%** of XSP's net assets. Currency forwards appear separately. For this example, approximate XSP's equity exposure as 100% U.S. large-cap and S&P 500 exposure, and assume the sample Canadian equity basket is 100% Canadian. Treat the currency hedge separately from the geographic exposure of the underlying equities. [BMO ZEQT semi-annual report, Schedule of Investments](https://bmo.bynder.com/m/7617d719b2a3e5e7/original/SA_FS_ZEQT_E.pdf) · [BlackRock semi-annual financial statements, XSP Schedule of Investments](https://www.blackrock.com/ca/investors/en/literature/semi-annual-report/sar-fs-equity-en-ca.pdf) · [XSP investment objective and underlying disclosures](https://www.blackrock.com/ca/investors/en/literature/semi-annual-mrfp/sa-mrfp-xsp-en-ca.pdf)

| Metric | A · Initial simulated portfolio | B · Move 10 percentage points from Canadian equities to XSP |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Allocation to Canadian equities / ZEQT / XSP | 40% / 50% / 10% | 30% / 50% / 20% |
| Canadian equity exposure | 52.35% | 42.35% |
| U.S. equity exposure | 34.65% | 44.65% |
| International developed / emerging markets | 8.30% / 4.70% | 8.30% / 4.70% |
| Four-region concentration HHI (sum of squared regional weights) | 0.4032 | 0.3878 |
| S&P 500 allocation (XSP weight + ZEQT weight × 45.51%) | 32.76% | 42.76% |
| Hypothetical scenario return (Canada −12%, U.S. −20%, international developed −15%, emerging markets −25%) | −15.63% | −16.43% |

Sum each portfolio holding's weight multiplied by that fund's geographic weights to obtain portfolio exposures. For fixed shocks, sum each regional weight multiplied by the corresponding shock. If only the U.S. shock changes and Canada stays at −12%, the difference in scenario returns between B and A is `0.10 × (U.S. shock − Canadian shock)`. At exactly −12% for the U.S. shock, **the two portfolios have the same scenario return**. B loses less when the U.S. decline is below 12% and more when it exceeds 12%. The four regional shocks are separately specified hypotheses. The illustration covers geographic equity shocks and leaves separate currency, fee, and liquidity shocks outside its scope. The holdings date establishes the exposure snapshot. Historical backtests must restrict available information using the actual publication date. [Bank of Canada scenario analysis example](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/publications/financial-stability-report/financial-stability-report-2026/how-a-financial-market-correction-could-worsen-the-effects-of-an-oil-shock/)

### Direct Evidence of Underlying Holdings

In the issuer tables for the same date, IVV, held through XSP, and ZSP, held through ZEQT, disclosed the same top 25 issuers. Taking the smaller displayed weight for each issuer and summing gives **51.7 percentage points of disclosed overlap**. The calculation uses each holding's weight in its underlying fund's own net assets. The published table rounds weights to 0.1 percentage points. The result covers the 25 checked issuers in the two underlying funds. Complete overlap and overall overlap between XSP and ZEQT require full holdings lists for the same date and common identifiers. The report can show the portfolio's S&P 500 allocation and observed holdings overlap between the underlying funds on the same screen, each with its denominator and coverage. ## Proposed Multi-Agent Roles and AWS Integration

Run the **Evidence Agent** and the **Investor Constraints Agent** in parallel. The **Analysis Agent** receives their structured results and calls Python tools. After deterministic validation, the **Explanation Agent** presents the verified outcome in a decision summary. Give each model agent distinct tasks, inputs, outputs, and tool permissions. The orchestration layer controls execution order, timeouts, and branches. Deterministic code handles calculations and verification. [AutoGen, COLM 2024](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/autogen-enabling-next-gen-llm-applications-via-multi-agent-conversation-framework/) · [Strands Graph](https://strandsagents.com/docs/user-guide/sdk/multi-agent/graph/)

| Role | Handoff the demo can show |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence Agent | Select holdings information from permitted public sources and return evidence IDs, observation and availability dates, denominator, currency, granularity, coverage, and missing items. |
| Investor Constraints Agent | Convert simulated goals, horizon, risk willingness, loss capacity, and ESG conditions into hard constraints or preferences. Mark unknowns explicitly. |
| Analysis Agent | Select applicable calculations, call tools such as `compare_exposures` and `run_scenario`, and return numeric results, contributions, conditions that change the outcome, and evidence IDs. |
| Explanation Agent | Use validated results to describe the data, reasoning, limitations, and conditions that could change the conclusion. |

**Engineering approach.** Use Strands Graph or a small Python orchestrator to define an explicit directed acyclic workflow, and use Bedrock Converse for model and tool calls. With Converse, the application executes the tool and returns the result with the same `toolUseId`. The competition workshop specifies `us-east-1`. Start by verifying a text response and a complete tool round-trip with `global.amazon.nova-2-lite-v1:0`. All agents share the team account's quotas. Bound timeouts, retries, and tool rounds. The workshop also provides the Codex on Bedrock development setup. AWS documentation states that **Bedrock Agents Classic stopped accepting new customers on 2026-07-30**. As an AWS alternative, the analysis could read a versioned public evidence snapshot from a private S3 bucket in the competition account, subject to permissions, data rights, and competition rules. [Competition AWS setup](https://catalog.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/event/dashboard/fr-FR/workshop/20-setup) · [Competition Codex guide](https://catalog.us-east-1.prod.workshops.aws/event/dashboard/fr-FR/workshop/40-quickstarts/60-ai-coding-assistant/30-codex) · [Bedrock client-side tool use](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock/latest/userguide/tool-use-client-side.html) · [Classic maintenance mode](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/us_en/bedrock/latest/userguide/agents-classic-maintenance-mode.html) · [S3 GetObject](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/API/API_GetObject.html)

**Explainability and failure handling.** Save the run ID, versions, tool inputs, results, rules, and evidence IDs. When the evidence dates, currencies, granularity, or versions are incompatible, flag the problem in validation and return a conditional result or request the required information. Check weights, units, data coverage, numbers, and citations. MAST identified 14 failure modes across three categories in multi-agent traces, covering system design, misalignment between agents, and insufficient verification. Kim and colleagues' preprint found that the benefit of an architecture depends strongly on whether the task can be decomposed. The two studies support explicit handoffs and independent verification. [Cemri et al., MAST, NeurIPS 2025](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/b1041e52d3be19f0a9bc491657488e4a-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html) · [Kim et al., arXiv v3, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.08296) · [NISTIR 8312](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.IR.8312) · [Turpin et al., NeurIPS 2023](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/ed3fea9033a80fea1376299fa7863f4a-Abstract-Conference.html)

## Prototype Acceptance Criteria

1. Reproduce the table values for both known allocations. Regional contributions must sum to the scenario return. Portfolio weights must sum to 100% before and after the trade.
2. Reordering holdings must preserve the risk assessment. Changing only the U.S. shock must reproduce the −12% crossover. Changing only Robert's scenario loss cap must change the outcome at the threshold.
3. Remove one key holding or change the snapshot date to verify that the report reflects coverage, missing data, and the resulting limitations.
4. Inspect each agent's tool calls, inputs, outputs, and handoffs. Use fixed test cases and record calculations, constraints, citations, and errors across at least three independent runs.
5. The team validated Nova 2 Lite text calls and a client-side tool round-trip in CloudShell and completed the official introduction notebook.
